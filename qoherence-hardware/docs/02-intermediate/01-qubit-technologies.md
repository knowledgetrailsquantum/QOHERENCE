# Qubit Technologies Compared (Intermediate)

| Technology       | Provider examples | Pros                          | Cons                       |
|-------------------|--------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Superconducting   | IBM, Rigetti       | Fast gates, mature tooling    | Short coherence, needs mK cooling |
| Trapped ion       | IonQ               | High fidelity, long coherence | Slower gates                |
| Photonic          | Xanadu             | Room temp, natural networking | Hard to do 2-qubit gates    |

Different technologies imply different noise profiles — relevant when
choosing mitigation strategies in qoherence-mitigate.
